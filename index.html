Cheat codes

Your console, your buttons

Pick PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series X|S once, and the app shows each code as the presses on your controller, in order. Each code also says what it does and what unlocks it. In GTA VI you can use cheats once you finish the story, and many need a challenge too.

How to enter one
Press Up on the D-pad to take out the phone, hold L1 and press R1 (LB and RB on Xbox), then enter the code. The app keeps these steps above the list.
Favorites first
Star the codes you use to keep them under Favorites. Type a name in the search field to find any other code.
No app update for new codes
We add new codes on our server, and you see them without updating the app. Turn on notifications and we’ll tell you when they arrive.

Read Aloud

Keep your eyes on the TV

Tap Read Aloud and look back at the game. After a short pause, a voice in your language calls out each press while a ring moves across the buttons, and you feel a tap for each one.

Three speeds
On Slow you have time to find each button. Pick the speed in Settings.
Works on silent
The voice plays when your iPhone is on silent, and the screen stays on until the code ends.

Siri and Shortcuts

Ask Siri, keep the controller

Say “Read the $250,000 code in Backseat” while you hold the controller, and the app opens the code and reads it out. Ask “What’s the Max Ammo code in Backseat” and Siri tells you the buttons. Say “Show cheat codes in Backseat” to open the list.

Codes by name
Siri and Shortcuts know every code in the app by name, and pick up new ones the next time you open it.
Spotlight too
Type a code’s name in Spotlight to open it in Backseat.

Widgets

Codes on your Home Screen

The Cheat code widget shows your favorites with the buttons for your console, or one code you pick. If you haven’t starred any, it shows a code of the day. The Countdown widget counts down to the next date we know of.

Lock Screen with Backseat Pro
Put a code under the clock, so it’s there when you pick up your iPhone. Pro is a one-time purchase. With it you can star more than three codes and read the trophy tips.

Trophies · new in 1.3

Every trophy, ticked off

All 55 trophies and achievements in one list, with the grade on PS5 or the Gamerscore on Xbox. Tick one off when it pops and watch the count climb. Story trophies name missions, so Backseat covers them until you tap one.

Tips with Backseat Pro
How to get the long ones, from 250 stunts to 100% completion, in a few lines with a link to the guide.
Cheats cost trophies
After a cheat you can’t earn trophies for the rest of the session, and the Cheats and Trophies tabs both remind you.

Questions

Is Backseat made by Rockstar?

No. Backseat is an unofficial fan app from an independent developer. It isn’t affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

How do I enter a cheat?

Finish the story first: cheats stay locked until then, and many also need a challenge from Progress › Cheats. Then press Up on the D-pad to take out the phone, hold L1 and press R1 (LB and RB on Xbox), and enter the code on the D-pad. After a cheat you can’t save or earn trophies for the rest of the session, so save before you start.

Can Siri read a code to me?

Yes. Say “Read the $250,000 code in Backseat”, or name another code, and Backseat opens it and reads out the presses. Ask “What’s the $250,000 code in Backseat” and Siri tells you the buttons. Codes we add later work too, once you open the app.

Do I need to update the app to get new codes?

No. The app loads codes, news and guides from our server when you open it, and Siri, Spotlight and the widgets get the new codes too. Pull down on any screen to check now.

Does it track my trophies?

From version 1.3, the Trophies tab lists all 55 trophies and achievements, and you tick them off as you earn them. Backseat doesn’t sign in to your PlayStation or Xbox account, so it can’t see which ones you have.

Which consoles does it cover?

PlayStation 5, including PS5 Pro, and Xbox Series X|S. Pick yours the first time you open the app and change it any time from the badge at the top of the screen.

Which widgets are there?

Two. Cheat code shows your favorites, a code you pick or a code of the day. It comes in three sizes for the Home Screen, and with Backseat Pro you can put it on the Lock Screen. Countdown counts down to the next date we know of.

What does it cost?

Backseat is free to download on the App Store. Backseat Pro is a one-time purchase with no subscription. It puts codes on your Lock Screen, lets you star more than three and opens the trophy tips.
