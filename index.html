Questions

Is Backseat made by Rockstar?

No. Backseat is an unofficial fan app from an independent developer. It isn’t affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

What are the numbers under some codes?

Some cheats also work through your character’s phone in the game. Open the phone, type the number on its keypad and call. The cheat turns on the same way as with the buttons. When a code works both ways, the app shows both.

Do I need to update the app to get new codes?

No. Codes, news and guides load from our server when you open the app. Pull down on any screen to check now.

Which consoles does it cover?

PlayStation 5, including PS5 Pro, and Xbox Series X|S. Pick yours the first time you open the app and change it any time from the badge at the top of the screen.

What does it cost?

Backseat is free to download on the App Store.
