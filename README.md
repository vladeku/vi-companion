# Backseat website

Privacy policy, terms of use and support page for Backseat, an unofficial fan app for Grand Theft Auto VI. Served by GitHub Pages from `main` at https://getbackseat.app/ (the `CNAME` file holds the domain).

- `privacy/` and `terms/`: the app's Settings and its App Store listing link to these two addresses.
- `support/`: the contact address; the listing's Support URL points here.
- `index.html`: the landing page: cheat codes, Read Aloud, Siri, widgets, trophies (from 1.3), news, guides, questions. `404.html`: any other address.
- `unlock-times/`: when GTA VI unlocks on PS5 and Xbox, in the visitor's own time zone, with the US and Canada table. The moments in its script follow `Shared/launch.json` in the app's repository; change both together. `assets/unlock-times/og.png` is its link preview, written by `scripts/social-unlock-times.py` there.
- `assets/landing/`: the phones on the landing (simulator captures in Apple's iPhone 18 Pro Max bezel, 840 px wide, top part only) and `og.png`, the link preview. Since 18 November 2026 they show the real GTA VI codes from version 1.3: Favorites with the Siri card and All with the note on entering cheats on PS5, Read Aloud on a press, Backseat's page in Shortcuts, the small and medium Cheat code widgets on Xbox, Trophies and News. Guides comes from 1.1.
- `assets/fonts/`: Anton, the icon's typeface, cut to Latin, under the SIL Open Font License (`OFL-Anton.txt`).

Backseat is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
