GTA VI unlock times

When you can play GTA VI

Both consoles unlock at midnight local time on Thursday, November 19. One exception: PlayStation Store in the US and Canada opens at midnight Eastern in every time zone.

US and Canada

PlayStation Store lists one moment for both countries: 05:00 GMT on November 19, which is midnight Eastern. Xbox unlocks at midnight in each time zone. So PS5 opens earlier the further west you are, and later than Xbox in Atlantic Canada and Newfoundland.

Everywhere else

PS5 and Xbox both unlock at 12:00 AM local time on Thursday, November 19. New Zealand goes first, at 11:00 GMT on November 18: that’s 6:00 AM Eastern and 3:00 AM Pacific.

Questions

Does it go by where I am or by my console?

By your console. The store region of your PlayStation or Xbox account and the time zone set on the console decide when the game unlocks.

Why does PS5 unlock before Xbox on the West Coast?

PlayStation Store opens the game at one moment for the whole US and Canada: midnight Eastern, which is 9:00 PM on Wednesday in Pacific time. Xbox waits for midnight in your own time zone, three hours later.

Can I download it before launch?

Yes. Pre-load opened on November 10 on both consoles, and the download is 212 GB. A boxed copy holds a download code, not a disc: enter the code and the pre-load starts.

Where do these times come from?

From Rockstar Support and the release time on PlayStation Store. If either changes, we update this page.

What is Backseat?

An unofficial fan app for iPhone. Before launch it counts down to the moment the game unlocks on your console, on your Lock Screen and in a Live Activity. After launch it shows cheat codes with the buttons for your controller.
