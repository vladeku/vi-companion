US and Canada

PlayStation Store and Microsoft Store both list one moment for the US and Canada: 05:00 GMT on November 19, which is midnight Eastern. The further west you are, the earlier in the evening the game unlocks.

Everywhere else

Both consoles unlock at midnight in the main time zone of your store’s country. Most countries have one time zone, so that’s your own midnight. Australia goes by Sydney, Brazil by Brasília, Mexico by Mexico City and Spain by Madrid. New Zealand goes first, at 11:00 GMT on November 18: that’s 6:00 AM Eastern and 3:00 AM Pacific.

Questions

Does it go by where I am or by my console?

By your store. PlayStation goes by the country of your account, Xbox by the region set on the console. Each store opens the game at one moment for the whole country.

Why does it unlock before midnight on the West Coast?

Both stores open the game at one moment for the whole US and Canada: midnight Eastern. In Pacific time that’s 9:00 PM on Wednesday, on PS5 and Xbox alike.

What about the New Zealand trick?

Some Xbox players switch the console region to New Zealand, where the store opens the game at 11:00 GMT on November 18. Nobody has confirmed that it works for GTA VI. Finish the pre-load first and keep your own store’s time as plan B. On PS5 the store follows your account’s country.

Can I download it before launch?

Yes. Pre-load opened on November 10 on both consoles and needs up to 212 GB. A boxed copy holds a download code, not a disc: enter the code and the pre-load starts.

Where do these times come from?

From the release times on PlayStation Store and Microsoft Store for each country, checked on November 17, and from Rockstar Support. If they change, we update this page.

What is Backseat?

An unofficial fan app for iPhone. Before launch it counts down to the moment the game unlocks on your console, on your Lock Screen and in a Live Activity. After launch it shows cheat codes with the buttons for your controller and reads them out press by press. You can ask Siri for a code or keep your favorites in a widget on the Home Screen.
